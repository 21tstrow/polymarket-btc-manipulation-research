Single source of truth for the manipulation investigation: what's been built, what it found, what was retracted, and what to do next — including what to do when the in-flight runs land. Read this first. Last updated 2026-06-12 (post label-integrity correction + 15m Jan–Mar backward replication).

## The question

Is there evidence that someone pushes BTC spot in the final seconds of a 5m Polymarket window to swing the settlement, profit on Polymarket, and is it one actor / group? (Q1 detect · Q2 cost · Q3 reversion · Q4 wallets — see `research_design.md`.)

## Current verdict (one paragraph)

**No detected manipulation; one anomaly standing — a pre-close edge class carried across periods by two named high-volume wallets.** All detection tests are null (no outcome-flipping crossings — the lone Mar–Apr crossing assist verified on-chain; no above-baseline reversion; no beneficiary cluster; no shared funding; within-market timing indistinguishable from random). The opportunity is economically real (~$20–140 of slippage vs four-figure prizes). **2026-06-12 correction (read `02_exports/btc5m_resolution_gap/findings.md` + the corrected `02_exports/btc5m_crop_persistence/findings.md`):** two data defects were found and fixed — 248 contested markets carried wrong winner labels (the Kraken-price fallback used when Gamma `finalPrice` was missing; Jan–Apr only; ~13% of fallback rows; 0 errors in gamma-labeled and 15m rows), and post-close fills (the close→oracle gap, median 28s) were silently folded into all wallet stats. The previously named durable core (`0xed86741e` et al.) was an artifact of both and is retracted. **What survives, recomputed pre-close with on-chain labels:** an edge crop of 12–21 wallets per cell (Jan–Feb $15K / Mar–Apr $96K / May–Jun $130K / 15m $48K pre-close profit-if-held; post-close ≈ −$3K), winning mid-window commitments placed 30s–5min before close at ~0.45 entries (the final-10s edge is ≈ 0). Cross-period persistence at volume concentrates in **`0x10c95474`** (the copy leader; +0.23/107k sh → +0.25/77k sh) and **`0x30be23d0`** (+0.21/54k → +0.17/95k) — the original push-concentration suspects, restored to the top of the list by the corrected data. May–Jun (the preregistered cell) had zero label errors. Remaining explanations: micro-drift prediction (legal) vs micro-drift causation (manipulation) — both below the price-path detection floor. Separating them needs the sub-second quote-state data (Oracle collector, running) and the forward preregistered test scored pre-close with on-chain labels. **15m Jan–Mar (landed 2026-06-12, on-chain labels for all 8,633 markets by construction):** the class replicates backward at the same size (53–54 strong window-dressers vs Apr–Jun's 51–54) and is the richest corrected cell yet ($391K pre-close profit-if-held vs Apr–Jun's $48K, matching a 5× richer prize environment), with a **4-wallet 15m durable core** spanning both periods — led by `0x45ca1731` (z=29.7→18.4, ~$23.7K, onset-null in both) — and edges fading into Apr–Jun like every other cell. Read `02_exports/btc15m_wallet_edge_jan1_mar31/findings.md`.

## Key findings (read these before trusting any single report)

1. **Five false-positive traps were found and disarmed.** (a) Winner-aligned flow is positive *by construction* (flow→price→winner), amplified by closeness. (b) *Any* entry mechanically "leads" a final-seconds push — the market-maker control led 99.7% of the time. (c) Leading the largest flow bucket is not leading the *move*: a reaction bot firing on the first tick beats the loudest bucket. (d) **Post-close fills**: trades in the close→resolution gap (median 28s) inflate share-weighted "edge" because penny fills buy 10–100× the shares per dollar; all wallet stats must be pre-close-only. (e) **Label integrity**: when Gamma `finalPrice` is missing the universe falls back to the Kraken last price, which mislabels ~13% of those micro-margin markets (248 contested markets, Jan–Apr only); winner labels must come from on-chain `ConditionResolution` payouts. Reports written before a trap was discovered may carry the trapped reading; retractions are flagged in-place.
2. **The onset test (the trap-(b)/(c)-proof version) is null for everyone.** No wallet enters during flatness before moves more than a random timestamp in its own markets would (best p=0.029 uncorrected across 19 wallets). `0x32ec633a…` and `0x4d766f62…` skew significantly *post*-onset = latency-arb-shaped.
3. **The anomaly that survives (corrected) is a pre-close edge class winning mid-window commitments in micro-margin markets.** Per cell, 12–21 wallets at z≥5 with $15K–$130K pre-close profit-if-held; pooled corrected-crop edge is +0.08 to +0.17 per share on bets placed 30s–5min before close at ~0.42–0.50 entries (≈ 0 in the final 10s and post-close). Cross-period persistence at volume = `0x10c95474` and `0x30be23d0`. Wins settle at-or-above the contested-universe median margin (~3.4 bps) — not the razor-thin below-baseline margins a settlement-pusher would manufacture — and the onset test says nobody's entries lead moves better than random. Prediction-shaped, not manufacture-shaped, but causation below ~1 bp remains undetectable by price-path methods.
4. **The bot economy explains most of the follower-shaped wallets benignly** (copy/sniper clients reacting to feeds — see `bot_economy_map.md`), and Polymarket's dynamic taker fee shows the platform diagnosed this population as latency arb. It does **not** explain the directional suspects (independent, don't pay bot fees, co-trade rather than copy).
